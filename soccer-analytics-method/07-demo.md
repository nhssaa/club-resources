# Worked demo: 5 minutes of a public game

Everything in this package was validated on openly licensed footage, no minors from any school we work with.

- Source: "Girls Varsity Basketball v. Randolph - December 27, 2025", Hazen Union (VT), published by Hardwick Community Television on Wikimedia Commons under CC BY 4.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Girls_Varsity_Basketball_v._Randolph_-_December_27,_2025.webm
- Setup: the first 5 minutes (768p/24fps), a close match for a fixed sideline phone at 8-10 ft.
- Result: a full coach-readable report - segment summary, 22-event timeline, 16-row shot log, defensive reads, rotation and pace, 3 takeaways, and an honest limits section - for about $0.02 of model time.
- Labels: WHITE/DARK plus jersey numbers only; guesses tagged [inferred]; the limits section called out that broadcast commentary helped attribution and gym footage will not have it.

Run the demo yourself before your first real game: download the clip, take the first 5 minutes, run `03-prompts.md`, and compare your output against the video. Attribution: Hardwick Community Television, CC BY 4.0.

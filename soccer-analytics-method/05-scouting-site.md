# The scouting site layout

One private site for coaches and players beats spreadsheet links. Layout that worked:

- Footage library: one page per game with the report, the clip index and links to the clips in private storage.
- Season themes: a running page of recurring patterns across games ("all goals conceded came from service into a crowded box").
- Opponent inventory: for each opponent, where their video lives (public stream, school platform, none) and when you last checked.
- Access: private, link sharing off, one folder or site for staff only. Players get the clips, not the raw opponent footage, unless the coach says so.

Keep everything off public repos: the method is public, the footage and reports are not.

# Cost guide

Measured on real runs, October 2026:

- 5-minute segment, one structured analysis pass on a fast video model: about $0.02.
- Full school game, 6 segments: about $1.82.
- Comparison point: a Veo camera subscription is $215/month. Two full seasons of weekly games with this method cost less than one month of that.

Free and cheap options:
- Start on a free API tier and a short segment to prove the workflow before spending anything.
- Object tracking (player trails, heat maps): Roboflow's free tier covers a game or two per month; beyond that, a Colab Pro month ($9.99) is the fallback.
- Upload footage to cloud analysis only with school and AD approval - minors are on camera.

Set a hard spend cap per game batch and get the club advisor's yes before any paid run.

# Capture: one camera that works

Tested setup for a first game:

- Phone: a recent iPhone or Android. Nothing special.
- Position: 8-10 ft elevation behind the scorer's table (indoors) or midline (outdoors). Height beats zoom.
- Settings: 1080p/30, ultra-wide lens. Battery pack attached, Do Not Disturb on.
- When jersey-number reads matter (basketball paint action, crowded boxes), switch to the 1x main lens. Ultra-wide loses numbers at the far end.
- Two phones: the free Blackmagic Camera app can sync a second angle if you want it later.
- Indoors there is no commentary track, so whistle and bench attribution is weaker than on broadcast footage. Say so in the report's limits section.

Check with your AD and the venue before filming. Some leagues restrict recording minors; a school letter or coach request usually settles it.
